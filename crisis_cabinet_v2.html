Tusler Risk Classification (Tusler, 2006)

🐯 Tiger
High Probability · High Impact
Most dangerous. Requires immediate, active response. Cannot be ignored.
🐊 Alligator
Low Probability · High Impact
Rare but catastrophic if triggered. Transfer or contingency plan essential.
🐶 Puppy
High Probability · Low Impact
Frequent, minor disruptions that compound over time. Mitigate early.
🐱 Kitten
Low Probability · Low Impact
Monitor and accept. Spending resources here wastes budget.

Response Strategies (PMBOK §11)

AVOID
Eliminate the threat by changing the plan. Best for Tigers with unacceptable impact.
MITIGATE
Reduce likelihood or impact. Best for Tiger and Puppy risks within your control.
TRANSFER
Shift consequence to a third party (insurance, contracts). Best for Alligators.
ACCEPT
Acknowledge and build reserves. Appropriate for Kittens and low-priority risks.

Scoring
