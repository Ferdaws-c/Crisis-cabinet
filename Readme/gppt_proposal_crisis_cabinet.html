Project Proposal: Crisis cabinet

Idea Brief
A simulation-based training for IT Project Management students where they must navigate a high-stakes 'Crisis Cabinet' scenario to recover a failing project. Players take the role of a recovery lead and must make critical decisions regarding resources, stakeholder communication, and technical debt under time pressure.
Identity & Vision
Theme: Navigating
Target Audience: IT Project Management students and professionals.
Success Vision: Players can identify and handle project risks across different phases (Executing, Monitoring, Closing) to reach the final project completion (CEO Room).
Proposal Score
Current Readiness: 600.7 / 800 (75%)
Theoretical Framework
PMBOK 7th Edition, Agile Principles, Flow Theory, and Self-Determination Theory.
